My Take

1056 is one of those side characters who becomes way more interesting once you actually track what he does.

He can look distracted in the middle of a fight, but that doesn't mean he's useless. When something actually happens, his reactions are pretty solid. His biggest thing is probably how closely he sticks with his squad, especially Dark Blue Tied Cameraman.

03

Story & Character

Cameraman 1056 first appears as the POV Cameraman in Episode 66, fighting alongside Dark Blue Tied Cameraman. By Episode 67, the two become part of the Infiltration Squad and head into the Alpha-Hills Labs.

Across Episodes 67 to 70, 1056 repeatedly stays with the group, keeps watch, escapes dangerous encounters and helps the others. He also reacts strongly when the squad believes the Titans have been killed, which gives him a little more personality than the average background Cameraman.

His final appearance is Episode 70. After being exposed to the Claw Wheeler Skibidi Toilet's red TV light, he dies. Later material shows that his body remained at the scene and was examined by a Cameraman 2.0.

04

Appearance

Basically a Normal Cameraman, but with a classic black coat, white undershirt and a distinctive blue tie. The tie has a dotted/rhombus-like pattern with white shapes and a small red rhombus-shaped opening.

05

Combat Profile

  • Good close-range reflexes for a Normal Cameraman.
  • Can become extremely aggressive when his allies are threatened.
  • Uses squad equipment effectively in combat.
  • Has shown enough awareness to watch corridors and guard against surprise attacks.
  • His best confirmed solo kill is a Jetpack Skibidi Toilet.
  • Against tougher enemies, his contribution is more about support, survival and teamwork than overwhelming power.

Why I Actually Like This Character

He's not a Titan, he's not some absurdly important powerhouse and he doesn't need to be. His appeal comes from the little things: sticking with his friend, keeping watch when everyone else is focused somewhere else, actually reacting when his teammates are in danger, and continuing to move with the squad through the labs. He's the kind of character where the more you look, the more there is to talk about.
